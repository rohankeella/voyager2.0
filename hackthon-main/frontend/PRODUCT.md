# Voyager

Voyager helps travelers discover destinations and plan trips around their activities, budget, preferred seasons, and travel pace. The existing product presents destination matches, saved places, and day-by-day itinerary planning.

The public landing page addresses travelers deciding whether to create a travel profile or browse destinations. Operators have a separate entry at `/operator/overview`; returning travelers can use `/login`.
